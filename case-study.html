PRODUCT DESIGN · REDESIGN CASE STUDY

Fundraising is hard.
Finding the right fit
shouldn’t be.

Upsilon connects founders with investors. This concept redesign reshapes the first impression around one clear promise: fewer, more relevant introductions, with the reasoning visible on both sides.

01 / AUDIT

What a new visitor has to figure out.

A heuristic review of the current Upsilon homepage as a first-time founder and investor. The notes below refer to the live page’s observed copy and page structure. Evidence is an annotated reconstruction of the reviewed section, not a captured screenshot. Validate these observations with product analytics and user interviews before making production decisions. View the public page ↗

01The promise competes with the product list
UPSILON Home · About · Features
Find. Figure. Fund.
Founders, investors, AI matching, dashboards, deal rooms…
What outcome do I get first?

Issue. Matching, grants, profiles, dashboards and deal rooms all vie for the opening narrative.

Why it matters. A time-poor visitor needs a concrete first outcome and a clear next step.

Evidence. Homepage hero and feature sections introduce several distinct product promises before the user journey is established.

02Both audiences enter through the same broad pitch
Building or investing?
The right opportunities start here.
Shared platform overview, then separate feature lists
Two jobs, one entry

Issue. Founder and investor actions exist, but the initial context is a general platform story.

Why it matters. Founders need confidence and a relevant path; investors need control and signal.

Evidence. Role-specific process content appears lower on the page after shared positioning and features.

03AI matching is asserted before it is explained
AI MATCH ENGINE
96% relevance score
Sector · stage · traction · profile quality
What made this a fit?

Issue. A high score and several criteria are shown without a user-facing explanation of weighting, recency, or uncertainty.

Why it matters. In fundraising, opaque scores can look like false precision and weaken trust.

Evidence. The live page promotes an AI relevance score but gives no immediate explanation of how to interpret an individual result.

04Social proof is not tied to a decision
MEET THE NETWORK
Founder profiles & testimonials
Profiles, company descriptions, selected success signals
How recent? How verified?

Issue. Founder stories and network language do not consistently distinguish verified platform members, current criteria, or illustrative examples.

Why it matters. Credibility depends on knowing what is real, current, and relevant to my decision.

Evidence. Network and founder-profile sections sit alongside broad claims, with limited visible freshness cues.

05The fundraising promise needs careful boundaries
AI-POWERED MATCHMAKING
Reach investors that matter
Personalized matches · intent-based connections
Interest is not investment

Issue. Matching and fundraising-progress language sits close to outcomes that a visitor could read as likely funding or investor availability.

Why it matters. A financially consequential journey must distinguish discovery, mutual interest, and a funding decision.

Evidence. Homepage promises combine investor discovery, deal progression and fundraising progress without an equally prominent qualification.

06Accessibility and hierarchy are difficult to verify
LONG SCROLLING HOMEPAGE
Many product stories in sequence
Cards · stats · dashboards · FAQ · more cards
What should I read next?

Issue. Numerous sections and compact UI copy create a long page with few strong decision checkpoints.

Why it matters. Visitors on small screens or assistive technology need scannable structure and clear orientation.

Evidence. The page moves through network, dashboard, matching, deal-room, FAQ and feature narratives; a full WCAG/accessibility audit remains to be done.

02 / USERS

Founders need focus. Investors need current evidence.

These are research hypotheses to validate, not interview findings. They shaped the first version of the entry points and matching explanation.

FOUNDER · JOB STORY

“Help me spend my raise time wisely.”

When I’m preparing a round with limited time and a small network, I want a short list of investors that fit my stage and thesis, with visible reasons and next steps, so I can make informed introductions without sending the same pitch everywhere.

INVESTOR · JOB STORY

“Show me the signal I can trust.”

When deal flow exceeds my capacity, I want to see teams aligned with my stated criteria and understand what information is current or missing, so I can decide whether to take a closer look without being pushed into a conversation.

Validation plan: interview 2–3 founders and 2–3 active investors; test whether the fit reasons change their confidence, which evidence they require, and what “request an introduction” should actually do.

03 / BRAND

A distinct voice, with color reserved for decisions.

The palette gives the product a measured voice: navy anchors information, vermilion marks action, and warm paper keeps long reads comfortable. The mark expresses three connected points of view: founders, investors, and a useful introduction between them.

04 / REDESIGN

Make the value clear, then make the fit legible.

The home page gives each audience a direct route and a specific promise. The inner flow shows the actual product decision: relevant criteria, visible evidence, currentness, and the limits of a match score.

Before · multiple product claims arrive before the core outcome.→After · a founder-first promise and a clear profile-building action.
Before · founder and investor needs share a broad entry story.→After · two audience cards surface different jobs and next steps.
Before · a match score is presented with limited explanation.→After · four visible fit signals plus a clear “guide, not a promise” boundary.
Before · profile stories need more context on verification and recency.→After · current thesis date and verified status appear next to the relevant match.
Before · discovery language sits close to implied funding outcomes.→After · interest is framed as a mutual introduction; funding remains an independent decision.
Before · long feature scrolls create weak scan points.→After · shorter sections, role-specific paths, visible labels, keyboard states, and mobile stacking.

06 / NEXT

What I’d validate before launch.

Prototype feedback should challenge the assumptions behind the design, not just its visual polish.

Are fit reasons enough to build trust?→Test explanation comprehension and confidence with founders and investors.
